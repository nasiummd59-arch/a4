1. Understanding Digital Cookies & Session Tokens

Cookies are compact alphanumeric text files and cryptographic tokens placed on your personal desktop computer, tablet, or mobile smartphone when you explore our digital atelier and bespoke galleries. Cookies enable our secure web servers to recognize your authenticated session, remember your customized monogramming initials in the live previewer, preserve your preferred visual theme (Imperial Onyx or Silk Alabaster), and ensure smooth page transitions.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, NobleBagCrest may utilize modern browser storage technologies, including HTML5 local storage, session storage, and security tokens to maintain uninterrupted concierge workflows across diverse luxury client devices.

2. Classifications of Cookies Deployed by NobleBagCrest

We classify the cookies utilized on our digital domain into distinct operational tiers:

  • Strictly Essential Functional Cookies: Necessary for core website navigation, secure salon reservation submissions, CSRF token security, and account authentication. The platform cannot function securely without these essential tokens.
  • Atelier Preference & Styling Cookies: Preserve your bespoke user configurations, such as dark/light atelier theme preferences, currency displays (USD, EUR, GBP), and active monogram preview selections.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Telemetry: Collect pseudonymized aggregate metrics regarding server response times, image loading speeds, and navigation flows to assist our technical team in maintaining high-speed digital performance.

We do not deploy invasive third-party commercial advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or behavioral data broker beacons designed to track your online activities across unrelated commercial websites.

3. Analytics & Measurement Governance

We utilize Google Analytics (configured with tracking tag ID G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate general visitor volume, international geographic origin of patron cohorts, and content engagement across our maroquinerie monographs. Google Analytics deploys first-party cookies to compile aggregated, non-personally identifiable statistical reports. Strict IP anonymization is enforced to safeguard client anonymity.

4. Patron Preference Controls & Granular Consent

Upon your first visit to NobleBagCrest, you are presented with our interactive Cookie Consent banner, permitting you to select which categories of non-essential cookies you authorize. You may adjust your choices at any time by accessing this protocol page or clearing your browser cache.

Your consent selections are stored for a duration of twelve (12) calendar months, after which our platform will prompt you to review and renew your preferences.

5. Managing Cookies Through Browser Configurations

Most modern web browsers (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge) offer comprehensive controls permitting you to inspect, restrict, block, or delete cookies at your discretion. Please note that disabling strictly essential cookies may impair core portal functions, preventing interactive access to the bespoke monogram studio and salon booking tools.

6. Local Storage, Cache Tokens & Security

For computationally intensive interactive tools—such as the Patina Aging Simulator, High-Resolution Leather Texture Zoom, and Live Monogram Foil Previewer—NobleBagCrest utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage. This enables your browser to cache heavy image datasets locally, providing instantaneous responsiveness and frictionless browsing.
