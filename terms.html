1. Scope & Binding Acceptance

These Terms of Bespoke Acquisition ("Terms", "Agreement") constitute a formal binding legal agreement between the acquiring patron ("Patron", "Collector", "Client", "You") and NobleBagCrest Haute Maroquinerie Atelier ("NobleBagCrest", "the Atelier", "Our Maison", "We"). By placing a bespoke custom commission, acquiring a piece from our seasonal capsule releases, reserving a private salon appointment, or utilizing our digital concierge, you unreservedly agree to be bound by every covenant detailed herein.

2. The Bespoke Custom Commission Protocol

Each bespoke creation is an individualized artistic endeavor. The commission sequence follows strict artisanal milestones:

  1. Design Consultation & Specification Sign-off: The patron and lead maroquinier agree upon hide selections, thread colorimetry, hardware metallurgy, and monogramming parameters.
  2. Leather Hide Allocation: Top 2% grade-A vegetable-tanned hides are reserved and hand-clicked in Florence.
  3. Artisanal Execution: Hand skiving, two-needle saddle stitching, and seven-layer edge lacquering are executed across 40 to 80 labor hours.
  4. Provenance Registration & Hallmark Strike: The final creation is hallmarked, entered into the master ledger, and presented in our signature cedar presentation box.

3. Tuition Deposits, Billing & Price Invariance

Bespoke custom commissions require a non-refundable artisanal commitment deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the total agreed commission price at the time of design specification sign-off. This deposit secures the physical hide allocation and reserves the dedicated labor hours of a master maroquinier.

The remaining balance of fifty percent (50%), along with any applicable regional luxury sales taxes or customs duties, is due upon completion and photographic presentation of the finished creation prior to dispatch. All price quotes provided in official atelier dossiers remain invariant and guaranteed against raw material fluctuations for ninety (90) calendar days from issuance.

4. Natural Leather Characteristics & Handcrafted Nuance

NobleBagCrest works exclusively with full-grain, unadulterated vegetable-tanned leathers. Minor organic variations in grain texture, subtle growth marks, slight tonal gradients, and natural pore distribution are not manufacturing defects; they represent the authentic hallmark of living organic material that has not been plasticized with synthetic polymer coatings.

Patrons acknowledge that two-needle hand saddle stitching exhibits subtle human variations that distinguish master hand-craftsmanship from sterile robotic machine sewing.

5. White-Glove Delivery & International Transit Risk

All finished creations are dispatched via insured, white-glove international courier services requiring direct adult signature upon receipt. Risk of loss passes to the patron upon physical delivery to the designated destination address. NobleBagCrest coordinates all export documentation, CITES permits (where applicable for exotic trims), and customs declarations.

6. Cancellation, Alterations & Return Protocols

Ready-to-Wear Capsule Creations: Capsule pieces acquired from our ready inventory may be returned in pristine, unworn condition with all protective hardware films, certificates of authenticity, and cedar boxes intact within fourteen (14) days of delivery for a full refund or atelier credit.

Bespoke & Monogrammed Commissions: Custom bespoke creations, pieces featuring personalized monogramming, or items crafted to non-standard dimensional specifications are strictly non-refundable and exempt from general cancellation rights once hand-cutting of hides has commenced.

7. The Lifetime Structural Warranty & Restoration Spa

NobleBagCrest provides a comprehensive Lifetime Structural Warranty to the original registered patron. This warranty covers:

  • Failure or unravelling of any two-needle hand-stitched linen seam.
  • Mechanical failure of solid brass turn-locks, hinges, or base studs.
  • Structural delamination of handle salpa cores or internal reinforcements.

The warranty does not cover normal cosmetic leather wear, water stains from liquid immersion, improper chemical cleaning, unauthorized third-party modifications, or physical damage resulting from pet accidents or severe abrasion. Patrons may submit bags to our Madison Avenue leather spa for routine re-conditioning and edge re-lacquering at standard guild service rates.

8. Intellectual Authorship & Design Rights

All silhouette blueprints, structural gusset geometries, hardware mechanisms, logos, crests, and published photography represent the exclusive intellectual assets of NobleBagCrest Atelier. Acquisition of a physical handbag confers ownership of the physical object but conveys no license to reproduce, commercialize, or replicate our proprietary patterns.

11. Atelier Force Majeure & Sourcing Delays

Because NobleBagCrest works exclusively with certified top 2% grade-A French and Tuscan full-grain hides, exceptional natural events, severe alpine climatic anomalies, or governmental biological quarantine protocols affecting regional European tanneries may occasionally delay raw hide shipments. In such rare instances, the Atelier will immediately notify the commissioning patron and provide an updated bespoke completion schedule.

Neither party shall be held liable for reasonable delivery delays resulting from acts of God, extreme meteorological events, civil disruptions, or international customs border inspections beyond the direct operational control of our Maison.

12. Code of Salon Decorum & Concierge Hospitality

Our private salons on Madison Avenue, Place Vendôme, and Via Montenapoleone are sanctuary spaces dedicated to the quiet appreciation of fine craftsmanship, artisanal excellence, and personalized consultation. We request that all attending patrons and guests observe dignified salon etiquette, arrive promptly for scheduled private appointments, and treat our master maroquiniers, apprentice artisans, and concierge hosts with mutual collegial respect.
